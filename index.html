Free screenshots and screen recording for Windows

Select an area, add a label or an arrow, and the picture is already on your clipboard. You can also record your screen to MP4 or GIF, with sound.

Screenshots with annotations

Press Ctrl+Alt+A and the screen freezes. Select the area you need, mark it up right there, then press Enter to copy it or Ctrl+S to save it to a file. Blur hides a password or a face.

  • SelectV
  • PenP
  • ArrowA
  • RectangleR
  • HighlighterM
  • TextT
  • BlurB
  • NumberingN

Pin it on top

Keep a screenshot as a small window above all others, for anything you need in front of you.

Change anything later

Move, recolor or delete any annotation. Double-click text to edit it. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.

Paste images in

Ctrl+V adds an image from the clipboard, and the toolbar button inserts one from a file.

Screen recording to MP4 or GIF

  1. Press Ctrl+Alt+R and select an area, or Ctrl+A for the whole screen.
  2. Turn on the microphone and computer sound if you need them.
  3. Press the red button or Enter. After a 3-2-1 countdown, recording starts.
  • MP4 or GIF
  • 15, 30 or 60 FPS
  • Microphone
  • System sound

Hotkeys

The defaults are below. Change any of them in the settings, and StopPic will tell you if another app already uses a shortcut.

Ctrl+Alt+ASelect an area and annotate it
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+ACopy the whole screen right away
Ctrl+Alt+WCopy the active window right away
Ctrl+Alt+RSelect an area and record it
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+RRecord the whole screen right away

Download and install

  1. On the Releases page, download StopPic_…_x64-setup.exe.
  2. Run it and choose English or Russian. You can change the language later in the settings.
  3. StopPic stays in the tray. Open its settings from the tray menu.

Questions

Is StopPic really free?

Yes. It's free, and its source code is open under the MIT license.

Does it send my screenshots anywhere?

No. StopPic sends nothing over the network. Screenshots, recordings and settings stay on your computer.

Where are my files saved?

Screenshots go to Pictures\Screenshots, and you can choose another folder in the settings. Clicking the notification opens the folder with the file.

The sound comes from the wrong device. What do I do?

Pick the microphone and output device in the "Screen recording" section of the settings. "Windows default" means the device chosen in system settings.

What do I need to run it?

Windows 10 or 11. If the Microsoft Edge WebView2 component is missing (it's usually already there), the installer downloads it automatically.
